I am a postdoc in CSE at the University of Michigan, hosted by Nikhil Bansal and Euiwoong Lee. I am interested in high-dimensional sampling, theoretical computer science, and more broadly, the foundations of machine learning and data science. My study was supported by Korea Foundation for Advanced Studies (KFAS).

Fall 2026: I’m at the Simons Institute at UC Berkeley for Spectral Theory Beyond Graphs. Feel free to reach out.

[Past visits] I received my Ph.D. in CS from Georgia Tech in 2026, advised by Santosh Vempala, and my B.S. in Math from KAIST in 2021. In Spring and Summer 2025, I was a student researcher at Google Research, supervised by Matthew Fahrbach.
